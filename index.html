Who is Michael Donnon?

Michael Donnon is a senior growth and marketing executive who scales consumer brands through their steepest growth curves — from streaming launches to subscription turnarounds to AI-powered hardware.

He led international demand marketing and user acquisition for Paramount+, launching the app in 8 new markets in 2 years using incrementality measurement, media mix modeling, and omnichannel performance marketing. Before that, he built Pantaya from a business plan into the #1 Spanish-language streaming service in the U.S., and pioneered in-house performance media at eHarmony in the early days of large data models — cutting customer acquisition costs 81%.

Today he's VP of Marketing at Whisker Labs — makers of Ting, the AI-powered fire-prevention sensor protecting 1.4M+ homes — where he built the company's MarTech and data stack from zero and deployed AI marketing automation across the org. He also advises startups on go-to-market strategy through MELT Labs. He's a builder first: data stacks, teams, brands, and the retention and lifetime-value systems that connect them.
